# HR rules
- All displayed vacation balances use the RLS-invoker hr_leave_balances RPC; configured openings override legacy carry-over/extra in their year and following years derive carry-over recursively, while unconfigured people retain legacy rules. Why: one reference-date calculation prevents inconsistent totals without altering requests or approvals.
- Approval inbox aggregates only server-authorized request types and uses existing leave/change RPCs plus a locked remote-work wrapper; counters count requests separately from vacation-day balances. Why: the inbox must not widen approval rights or double-deduct leave.

- Changes to leave (vacation_requests) after approval go only through the DB functions leave_change_submit / leave_change_decide / leave_direct_change ; new pending requests are approved/rejected only via leave_decide_pending (trigger vacation_request_decision_guard_trg blocks direct writes) (ownership, no self-approval, no overlaps, history in vacation_request_history, bell notifications); cancelled leave becomes estado "cancelada", never deleted; emails follow via sendLeaveChangeEmails. Why: the existing timesheet sync trigger rebuilds leave hours from the request, so one write path keeps balance, timesheet and hours bank consistent.
- Approved certified sick leave (baixa_medica) needs attachment_path (CHECK chk_baixa_medica_requires_attachment); normal submission requires a certificate and emails EMAIL_CONTABILIDADE and Tatiana separately via notifyBaixaMedicaSubmitted (email-js has no CC). Why: approved sick leave must be certified and accounting must get the certificate at once.
- Service-only admin reconciliation uses leave_admin_reconcile_preserving_timesheets with a verified admin actor, exact before rows and history; it preserves every timesheet row and rejects affected approved weeks pending explicit bank reconciliation. Pending sick leave may lack a certificate, but approval still requires it. Why: reconciliation must not rewrite locked or unlocked actual hours or silently change weekly approval decisions.

- Hours bank (src/lib/hr/use-hours-bank.ts): balance is always the ledger sum; one opening_balance per person (edits audited in pm_hours_bank_audit) and at most one week movement (additional_hours credit or shortfall debit, replaced on re-approval); week movements for weeks ending before the opening "as of" date have no effect; unpaid leave ("Authorized (unpaid)") is shown but never accounted (isUnpaidLeave). Why: history is covered by the opening balance and unpaid leave is time owed.
